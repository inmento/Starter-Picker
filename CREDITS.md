# Credits

## Gen 1 Randomizer interoperability

**Wes_Kestis** is credited as the creator of **Gen 1 Randomizer**. Starter Picker’s targeted interoperability with that mod was implemented after reviewing the Randomizer’s starter-related behavior with Wes_Kestis’s permission.

Wes_Kestis granted permission for this community-focused compatibility work in a Discord conversation on August 14, 2026. Thank you to Wes_Kestis for the permission, support, and commitment to the Gen 1 Recomp modding community.

Gen 1 Randomizer remains an independent mod by its respective creator. This credit acknowledges the compatibility work only and does not imply a joint release or wider endorsement.
