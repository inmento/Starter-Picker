# Credits

## Gen 1 and Gen 2 Randomizer interoperability

**Wes_Kestis** is credited as the creator of **Gen 1 Randomizer** and **Gen 2 Randomizer**. Starter Picker’s targeted interoperability with both mods was implemented after reviewing their starter-related behavior with Wes_Kestis’s permission.

Wes_Kestis granted permission for this community-focused compatibility work in Discord conversations on August 14, 2026, including renewed permission to review the Gen 2 Randomizer for Starter Picker v0.0.4. Thank you to Wes_Kestis for the permission, support, and commitment to the Gen 1 Recomp modding community.

Gen 1 Randomizer and Gen 2 Randomizer remain independent mods by their respective creator. This credit acknowledges the compatibility work only and does not imply a joint release or wider endorsement.
